How to use RSS feeds?

RSS (Really Simple Syndication) allows you to aggregate updates from your favorite blogs and sites in a single interface, without algorithms or email newsletters tracking your views.

To subscribe:

  1. Get a feed reader application (e.g., NetNewsWire, Feedly, Feedbin, Inoreader, or NewsBlur).
  2. Copy one of the feed links below.
  3. Paste the link into your reader's "Add Feed" or "Subscribe" dialog.
