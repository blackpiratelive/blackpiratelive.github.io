How to use RSS feeds?

RSS (Really Simple Syndication) allows you to aggregate updates from your favorite blogs and sites in a single interface, without algorithms, ads, or email newsletters tracking your activity.

To subscribe:

  1. Get a feed reader application (e.g., NetNewsWire, Feedly, Feedbin, Inoreader, NewsBlur, or FreshRSS).
  2. Copy one of the feed URLs below.
  3. Paste the link into your reader's "Add Feed" or "Subscribe" dialog.

Available Feeds

1. Unified Feed

Combines both blog posts and microblog thoughts into a single aggregated timeline.

2. Blog Feed

Contains only complete long-form blog posts with full HTML content embedded directly in the feed.

3. Thoughts Feed

Contains microblog thoughts and updates where the feed item's title is the thought content.

4. Atom Feed

Standard Atom 1.0 XML syndication format for readers that prefer Atom.
